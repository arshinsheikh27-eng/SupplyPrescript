# Optimization & Alternative Actions

## Objective

Recommend the most suitable action when a shipment has a high risk of late delivery.

## Prediction Inputs

- order_id
- late_delivery_risk
- prediction_probability

## Possible Actions

1. No Action
2. Monitor Shipment
3. Alternate Supplier
4. Air Freight

## Provisional Demo Constraints

These are demo assumptions for prototype development only.

- Air Freight Premium = Configurable
- Alternate Supplier Cost = Configurable
- Budget Per Order = Configurable
- Target Service Level = Configurable
- Minimum Inventory Requirement = Configurable
